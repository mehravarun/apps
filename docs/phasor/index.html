About PHASOR

PHASOR is a pocket toolkit for radio-frequency engineers, students and hobbyists. It brings together the calculations you reach for every day on the bench, and it runs entirely in your browser, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

The Convert tab turns any power or voltage (dBm, dBW, watts, Vrms, Vpk, Vpp, dBµV, dBmV) into all the others at the impedance you choose. Next to it, a mismatch converter moves between VSWR, return loss, reflection coefficient, S11, mismatch loss and reflected power, with a meter showing how much power actually reaches the load.

The Smith tab is an interactive Smith chart. Drag the load point around, or type its resistance and reactance, and PHASOR solves every two-element L-network that matches it to your system impedance. It gives real inductor and capacitor values at your frequency and draws each matching path on the chart.

The Cascade tab works out a receiver chain's total gain, noise figure (Friis), input and output IP3, sensitivity and spurious-free dynamic range. It also shows how much of the total noise each stage contributes. The Link tab builds a free-space link budget with a waterfall chart, fade margin, maximum range and Fresnel-zone radius. Your values are saved on your device.

How to use it

  1. Pick a tab: Convert, Smith, Cascade or Link.
  2. Type your values. Every result updates as you type.
  3. On the Smith chart, drag the load point and tap a match solution to see its path.
  4. Tap any result tile to copy its value.
  5. Open the '?' notes under each panel for the formulas behind the numbers.
