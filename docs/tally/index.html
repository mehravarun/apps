About Tally

Tally is a simple, flexible tracker for anything you want to count or time: dog walks, cups of coffee, practice sessions, meetings, workouts or chores. Each event can be a counter (tap to log one), a timer (start and stop a session) or both.

The twist is that events can be nested. Put "Dog walk" under "Pets", or give each family member their own sub-event, and Tally rolls the totals up the tree, so you see both the detail and the big picture. You can move events to a new parent at any time, and running timers are highlighted so you never forget one.

The History tab shows totals for today, this week, this month or any period, broken down by event. You can edit or delete any entry if you logged something by mistake. Everything is saved privately on your own device. There's no account and no server, and nothing is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Open Events and add something to track, choosing counter, timer or both.
  2. Nest events under a parent to get roll-up totals.
  3. On Track, tap +1 to count or Start/Stop to time a session.
  4. Open History to see totals by period and edit any entry.
