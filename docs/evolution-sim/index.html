About Evolution Sim

Evolution Sim is an artificial-life sandbox where natural selection plays out in front of you. Each organism carries a genome that sets its body size, speed, senses, lifespan and diet. They graze on plants, hunt each other, mate with genetically similar partners and pass on mutated copies of their genes. Nothing about species is scripted: the simulator finds species by clustering genomes, and colours them as they appear.

Over a few minutes you'll see niches form. Plant eaters and meat eaters drift apart, body sizes split, hunters rise and crash with their prey, and lineages branch off and go extinct. A live family tree shows every species over time, a diet-versus-size chart shows the separate niches, and population charts track organisms, hunters and plant biomass. Lifetime stats cover births, deaths by cause, generations and hunting success.

You control the world: its size (up to 3,600 organisms), sim speed or max speed, mutation rate, plant growth, mating range, seasons, and two energy-cost formulas, including a realistic Kleiber-law option. An optional genome layer adds chromosome linkage, regulatory genes and gene duplication. Use a seed to repeat a run exactly, and copy the log as CSV to analyse it yourself.

How to use it

  1. Let the simulation run and watch colours (species) appear.
  2. Use the family tree and diet-vs-size charts to see species split into niches.
  3. Change mutation, plant growth or mating range, then press Reset to compare.
  4. Switch on seasons or the genome layer for more complex runs.
  5. Set a seed to repeat a run, and use Copy log as CSV to export data.
