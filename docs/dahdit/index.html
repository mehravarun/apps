About Dahdit

Dahdit is a complete Morse code toolkit that teaches you to hear code, not count dots. It's named after the rhythm of the letter D: dah-di-dit.

The Learn tab uses the Koch method, the approach many radio amateurs swear by. You start with just two characters played at full speed, tap the letter you heard, and a new character is added each time you reach 90% accuracy. Farnsworth timing lets you keep each character fast while adding extra space between them, so you learn the sound of letters rather than counting beeps.

The Key tab turns your screen (or the space bar) into a telegraph key. Tap for a dit, hold for a dah, and Dahdit decodes what you send in real time, adapting to your speed and showing your WPM. The Translate tab turns any text into Morse code and plays it as audio, and the Chart tab lets you tap any character to hear it. You can set character speed, effective speed, tone pitch and volume. It follows the international Morse standard (ITU-R M.1677) and runs entirely in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Open Learn and press Start, then tap the letter you heard.
  2. Reach 90% accuracy to unlock the next character.
  3. Use Key to practise sending: tap for a dit, hold for a dah.
  4. Type text in Translate and press Play to hear it.
  5. Adjust speed, tone and volume in Settings.
