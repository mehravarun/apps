About Mycel

Mycel is a calm, glowing take on the classic rotate-the-pipes connection puzzle. The board is a patch of forest floor crossed by strands of fungus. A spore sits in the middle, and mushrooms are scattered at the ends of the strands. Every tile has been turned at random, and your job is to rotate them back until every mushroom draws food from the spore.

Tap a tile to turn it a quarter clockwise. Strands that connect back to the spore light up amber, so you can watch the network grow as you solve it. Long-press (or right-click) a tile you're sure of to pin it so you can't turn it by accident. When the last mushroom is fed, a wave of light ripples outward across the board.

Choose a 5×5 Patch, 7×7 Grove or 9×9 Forest. Daily mode gives everyone the same board each day, and Wild mode makes a fresh random board whenever you like. Your best time and move count for each size are saved on your device. There's no sign-up and no ads, and it works on phones and desktops.

How to use it

  1. Tap a tile to rotate it clockwise.
  2. Connect strands back to the glowing spore in the middle. Connected strands light up.
  3. Long-press or right-click a tile to pin it in place.
  4. Feed every mushroom to finish the board.
  5. Switch size, or press New for a random Wild board.
