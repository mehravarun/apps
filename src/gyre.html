Gyre·

A Euclidean rhythm orbit. Four rings spread their beats as evenly as math allows, then drift against each other.

Tap any dot to flip that step · Space to play

Swing
Each ring plays E(k, n): k hits spread over n steps as evenly as possible, the same rule behind the Cuban tresillo E(3,8), the cinquillo E(5,8) and the West African bembé bell E(7,12). Rings with different step counts loop at different lengths, so the pattern shifts every bar until they line up again. Rotate slides a pattern around its ring.
